## Spec-Kit

This repository follows the [spec-kit](https://github.com/github/spec-kit) workflow.

- `.specify/templates/` — spec, plan, task and checklist templates.
- `.specify/memory/` — long-lived context such as `constitution.md`.
- `.specify/scripts/` — helper scripts.
- `.specify/hooks.yml` — hook definitions.
